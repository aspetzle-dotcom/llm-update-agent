      How to read this

      • Backtest: each model is trained without the most recent periods, then asked to predict them. That is the fairest test of how it will do on the future.
      • Typical % error (MAPE): the average miss as a percentage of the actual value. “±6%” means forecasts were usually within about 6%.
      • MAE / RMSE: the average miss in your own units; RMSE punishes big misses more.
      • Bias: positive means the model tended to over-forecast; negative, under-forecast.
      • Ranges: the shaded bands show where the actual value is likely to land 80% or 95% of the time, if the future behaves like the past. They are approximations and widen further out.
      • Limits: these are statistical models of history. They cannot know about launches, price changes or market shocks unless you add them as a scenario.
